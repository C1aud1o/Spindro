Spindro is not medical advice. Ask a doctor before starting hard training, especially if you have health conditions. Stop if you feel unwell. Check that your bike and trainer are set up and fixed safely. Resistance changes made by the app, such as ERG mode, can be sudden. Use the app only in a safe place.
