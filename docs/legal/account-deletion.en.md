# Deleting your data

*Last updated: 15 November 2026*

Spindro keeps your data on your device. The account is optional: if you don't create one, we hold nothing about you.

**In the app (easiest).** Open Settings → Data and diagnostics → **Delete all my data** and confirm. This erases from the device: history and records, settings, workouts you created, and the Strava link (access is also revoked). It cannot be undone: export first with "Export my data" if you need it. It also works with only the Zwift Click buttons.

**If you have an account.** The profile and cloud data are deleted from Settings → Account → **Delete account**; the app then asks whether to also erase the data on the device. "Delete all my data" only acts on this device.

**Without opening the app.**
- **In the browser:** clear the site data for the Spindro site in your browser settings.
- **On Android:** open Settings → Apps → Spindro → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app.

Rides already uploaded to Strava are managed in Strava (you can also revoke access in your Strava settings).

If a Spindro trainer report on GitHub contains something you want removed, contact GitHub or ask in the issue: https://github.com/C1aud1o/Spindro/issues

See also the [Privacy Policy](privacy.en.md).
