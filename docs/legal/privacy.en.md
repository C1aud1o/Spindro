# Spindro Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 15 November 2026*

Spindro is a free, open source app for smart trainers. It runs in the browser and as an Android app. It is maintained by the Spindro project, an open-source project maintained by volunteers.

## In short

- **Spindro has no accounts and no servers of its own.** We do not collect, receive or store any personal data.
- We count visits **anonymously**, with no cookies. In the app, only if you say yes.
- Your rides, settings and trainer data stay **on your device**.
- The website has **no cookies** and does not follow you from one site to another.
- The only connections to other companies start from **you**, and they are listed below.

## What stays on your device

The app saves these on your device only (browser storage, or the app's storage on Android): your settings, language, FTP and maximum heart rate, ride history, workouts, game records and the trainers you used before. We cannot see them.

If you use the Android app, the system may include this data in your Google backup (`allowBackup` is on). That is a choice of your device, not of Spindro.

## Bluetooth and sensors

The app talks to your trainer, power meter, heart-rate strap and Zwift controllers over Bluetooth. This data is used live in the app and saved only in your ride history on your device. It is not sent to us. On Android 11 and older, the system asks for the location permission only because Android requires it for Bluetooth scanning. Spindro does not read your location.

## What the app loads from the internet

- **Fonts from Google Fonts.** The app loads the Barlow fonts from Google (`fonts.googleapis.com`, `fonts.gstatic.com`). Google receives your IP address and the technical data of the request.
- **The website itself** is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log standard technical data of a request, such as the IP address.
- **Updates.** The app code is loaded from the same site.

## Anonymous statistics

To understand how many people use Spindro we count visits with **GoatCounter** (`goatcounter.com`), an open-source service that uses no cookies and stores nothing on your device.

- **On the website** the opened page is counted, with generic technical data: referring page, browser and system type, screen size, country. The IP address is not stored.
- **In the app** counting starts **only if you agree** when you first open it. You can change your mind in Settings → Privacy. These are counted: app opening, language, trainer protocol type (for example FTMS), game opened, ride completed. Never power, heart rate, location or device names.

We cannot trace this data back to you.

## Services you choose to use

These start only when you act. They have their own privacy policies.

- **YouTube.** If you start a video ride, the player is loaded from YouTube. YouTube may set cookies and collect data. See Google's policy.
- **Strava.** If you connect Strava, you use "personal mode": you enter your own Strava API credentials, and the app talks directly to Strava. Your ride file (`.FIT`, which includes heart rate if you used a strap) goes from your device to Strava. The access token stays on your device. Spindro's project has no server in between. You can disconnect in the app and revoke access in your Strava settings.
- **GitHub.** If you tap "Report your trainer", the app opens a pre-filled GitHub issue in your browser. It contains the trainer's Bluetooth name, services, protocol, platform and app version. Nothing is sent until you press submit on GitHub. GitHub issues are public.

## Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to give, correct or delete. To remove your local data, clear the site data in your browser, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. For data held by Google, Strava, YouTube or GitHub, contact those companies.

## Minimum age

Spindro is not meant for children under 14.

## Security

All connections use HTTPS. The code is public, so anyone can check what the app does: https://github.com/C1aud1o/Spindro

## Changes

If this changes, for example if accounts or statistics are added, we will update this page and its date **before** the change goes live.

## Contact

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/C1aud1o/Spindro/issues. For private matters, use GitHub's private security reporting on the same repository. Please do not post personal data in public issues.
